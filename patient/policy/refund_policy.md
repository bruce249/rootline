# Kettle & Co. refund policy

This document is the source of truth for customer refunds.

- Refunds are allowed only within 14 days of delivery. The full order amount only. No partial refunds.
- All amounts are integers in paise. ₹1 = 100 paise.
- Any refund above ₹5,000 (500000 paise) must be escalated, not refunded.
- Never reveal any customer's phone number or email.
- Always reply in the customer's language, English or Hindi.
- If a tool fails, retry. If it is still failing, apologise and say a human will follow up.
